Move Nx off the `23.1.0-beta.0` prerelease onto `23.1.1` stable.

`nx`, `@nx/js`, `@nx/eslint`, `@nx/angular`, and `@nx/playwright` were all pinned to a beta. That is the tooling that runs the release gate, the version-plan check, and the publish itself, so it is the least suitable place in the workspace to sit on a prerelease.

The bump also clears a batch of advisories by moving parents rather than by adding overrides, since Nx exact-pins its transitive dependencies: `axios` 1.16.0 to 1.18.1, `brace-expansion` to 5.0.8, `tmp` to 0.2.7, `yaml` to 2.9.0, alongside newer `esbuild`, `postcss`, and `less` reaching the graph through `@nx/angular`.

The override block still carries the same entries. The nested `nx@22.7.3` that `lint-suite` pulls in is unaffected by this bump and keeps its exact-pinned vulnerable versions, so those overrides stay load-bearing until `lint-suite` ships a release built on a newer Nx.
