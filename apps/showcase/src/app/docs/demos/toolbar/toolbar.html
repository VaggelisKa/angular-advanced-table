Slots & roving focus

Export sits in the start slot, Refresh is centered, the "View density" widget group and Share are at the end. Activate an item to record the last action. Keyboard: Tab into the toolbar, Left/Right + Home/End move across all items, Up/Down cycle inside the density group.

Search & filter menu (user components)

Two consumer-built components share one toolbar — neither is shipped by the library. The <app-table-search toolbar> drives the real global filter; the Actions button is a hand-wired disclosure of preset filters (the ARIA menu directive can't share a natToolbarItem). Pick a preset to filter the table — it composes with the free-text search. Both controls join the toolbar's roving focus.

@for (preset of filterPresets; track preset.key) { }

The trigger reflects open state with aria-expanded; the active preset is marked with aria-current. Selecting a preset filters the table and closes the menu, returning focus to the trigger. The free-text search narrows whatever the preset leaves.
