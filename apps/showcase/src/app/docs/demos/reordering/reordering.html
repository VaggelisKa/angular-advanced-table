Keyboard: focus a header cell, then press Ctrl + Shift + Left Arrow or Ctrl + Shift + Right Arrow to swap columns. On macOS, use Command instead of Ctrl.

Pointer: open a header actions menu and choose Move left or Move right.

Reorder by default: with [enableReordering]="true" on the surface, every column reorders. Opt one out with meta: { reorderable: false }. Every column above participates.
